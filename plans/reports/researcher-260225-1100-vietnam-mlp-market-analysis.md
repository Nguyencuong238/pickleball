## 6. Regulatory Landscape

### Consumer Protection Laws (Effective July 1, 2024)
- **Law No. 19/2023/QH15:** Updated Consumer Protection Law (replaces 2010 version)
- **Decree No. 55/2024/ND-CP:** Consumer rights in digital environment, vulnerable group safeguards
- **Key requirements for loyalty programs:**
  - Clear disclosure of reward terms, expiration policies, redemption conditions
  - Prevention of misleading practices
  - Transparency in benefits communication

### Data Protection Mandates
- **Vietnam Data Protection Law (2023):** Stricter controls on customer data collection/storage/processing
  - Loyalty platforms must implement secure data infrastructure
  - Consent-based data processing required
  - No transfer to unauthorized third parties

### Compliance Triggers for Loyalty Platforms
- Terms & conditions must be transparent, in Vietnamese (regulatory language accepted)
- Expiration dates on points must be disclosed upfront
- Data handling privacy policy required
- Consumer dispute resolution mechanism needed
- Refund/cancellation policies for unused rewards

### No Specific Loyalty-Points Tax/Regulation Found
- No evidence of VAT on reward points or special loyalty tax
- Points treated as customer benefit (not income) unless redeemed for cash
- Cashback schemes subject to standard VAT/business tax

## 8. Actionable Insights for Pickleball Court MLP Design

### High-Priority Features (Must-Have)
1. **Points-based earning:**
   - 1 point per 1,000 VND spent (or 1 point per court booking)
   - Bonus points for referrals (e.g., 50 points when friend books first court)
   - Double points on off-peak bookings (drive utilization)

2. **Tier-based progression:**
   - **Bronze:** 0-100 points/month (baseline)
   - **Silver:** 100-300 points/month (5% court discount)
   - **Gold:** 300+ points/month (10% discount + priority booking + monthly bonus)
   - Visual progression & milestone notifications

3. **Integrated payment (MoMo-first):**
   - One-tap checkout linking court booking → MoMo payment → points credit
   - Real-time points balance post-transaction
   - Transaction history visible in app

4. **Referral mechanics:**
   - Generate unique referral code per user
   - Referrer gets 50 points; referee gets 20% discount on first booking
   - Zalo/Facebook sharing native integration

### High-Impact Secondary Features
1. **Gamification:**
   - Weekly challenges (e.g., "Book 3 courts this week" → 30 bonus points)
   - Leaderboard (monthly/seasonal top bookers, community visibility)
   - Achievement badges (First booking, Referral master, Marathon player)

2. **Personalization:**
   - Recommend court times based on booking history
   - Birthday/anniversary 2x points bonus
   - AI suggest "friends' preferred courts" (social graph)

3. **Redemption options:**
   - 150 points = 50K VND court discount (easy redemption)
   - 500 points = free court booking (aspirational)
   - Partner benefits (e.g., racket rental discount, refreshment vouchers)
   - Points never expire (or 2-year policy, clearly stated)

4. **Community features:**
   - In-app friend list (find people to play with)
   - Court booking group messaging
   - Monthly league/tournament sign-ups with bonus points

### Regulatory Compliance Checklist
- [x] Terms & conditions in Vietnamese covering point expiration (if any), earning/redemption
- [x] Privacy policy for user data collection (booking history, payment method)
- [x] Transparent redemption exchange rates (publicly displayed)
- [x] Dispute resolution contact (email/hotline for point issues)
- [x] Data security certification (SSL, encrypted storage)
- [x] Explicit opt-in consent before promotional email/SMS
